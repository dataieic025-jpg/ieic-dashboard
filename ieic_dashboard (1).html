About IEIC

What is IEIC?

The International Electromedical Innovation Competition (IEIC) is an international-scale event organized by Poltekkes Kemenkes Surabaya in collaboration with APTEMI, IKATEMI, and HIMATEMI. This competition serves as a platform for students and young innovators around the world to showcase their creative ideas and innovations in the field of biomedical engineering and healthcare technology.

In Collaboration With:

Purpose

IEIC aims to foster creativity, collaboration, and innovation among biomedical technology students. Through this event, participants are encouraged to develop practical solutions that contribute to the advancement of medical technology in Indonesia and beyond.

Background

As healthcare technology continues to evolve rapidly, there is a growing need for innovative solutions in medical instrumentation and patient care. IEIC was established to bridge academic knowledge with real-world medical challenges, inspiring students to become pioneers of the future.
